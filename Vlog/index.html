June 1st, 2026 · 12:34 AM
I've been reading Rayuela by Julio Cortázar lately, and honestly, it has completely blown my mind. Every few pages I find myself stopping just to think about what I read. The way Cortázar plays with language, ideas, and structure feels unlike anything else I've encountered before.

Sometimes Rayuela feels more like wandering through someone's thoughts than reading a traditional novel, and that's exactly what makes it so fascinating. It's confusing, beautiful, strange, and brilliant all at the same time.

Definitely one of the most memorable books I've picked up in years. 📖
